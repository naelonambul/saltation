## Risks and mitigations

The load generator can stall on a saturated bank or under a tighter CPU cap. Preserve arrival and dispatch evidence and classify insufficient delivery as incomplete. Keep verification after drain and use read-only scope so it cannot manufacture the result. Separate the load-test verdict from the existing generator's live traffic and use a controlled run boundary so unrelated activity cannot contaminate the measurement.

## Rollback or recovery

Revert the harness and pool instrumentation together if deployment is blocked. Retain recorded evidence under its original revision and treat old smoke-style reports as exploratory. Resume qualification with a new run ID after fixing an incomplete collection or a workload failure.
